What I do

Cloud architecture

Cloud-native platforms designed for scale, cost and reliability. I took PLEXOS simulations to thousands of parallel jobs with autoscaling and usage-based pricing.

Gen AI development

AI agents grounded in real data, plus the eval harnesses that keep them honest. I build agents, tools and multi-agent workflows that turn complex models into answers people can act on.
